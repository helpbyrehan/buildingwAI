How the coach reads

Careful feedback.
No made-up certainty.

The coach gives feedback on prompt fit, specific evidence, reflection, voice, structure and clarity. The site can use either a rubric-based cloud model or a locally hosted Qwen model fine-tuned on a small synthetic UK/US practice set. Local feedback is experimental and has no validated overall grade. AI feedback can miss context, so weigh it against your own judgment and a trusted reader’s advice.

It gives writing advice, not admission chances, an official grade, a plagiarism verdict, or a rewritten essay. Check each college’s AI policy before using feedback.

The six review criteria

Prompt alignment — Does the draft answer the actual question?

Specific detail — Do actions and details support what you claim?

Reflection & insight — Does the reader understand why this matters to you?

Personal voice — Can the reader hear a credible individual?

Structure & focus — Do the parts move a clear idea forward?

Clarity & economy — Does the language make your meaning easier to understand?
