About AP Study Hub

A simple, focused home for AP students to discover and share useful study materials — built and maintained by students, for students.

🔎

Discover

Search and filter organized resources instead of digging through scattered folders, group chats, and dead links.

📤

Contribute

Students can submit PDFs or external resources. Every submission is moderated by an admin before it becomes public.

🗂️

Organize

Resources are tied to subjects, courses, units, years, and types so the library stays useful as it keeps growing.

How it works

Everything on AP Study Hub follows the same simple lifecycle, from submission to publication.

1. SubmitA signed-in student adds a PDF or external link with course, unit, and type.
2. ReviewAn admin checks the resource in the moderation dashboard before it goes live.
3. PublishApproved resources appear instantly in Browse, Courses, and search.
4. SaveAnyone can star a resource to keep it in their personal Saved list.
