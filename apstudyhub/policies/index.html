Privacy. Safety.
Community.

Plain-language expectations for using AP Study Hub responsibly.

Privacy

AP Study Hub stores account information, selected courses, study progress, saves, practice results, class membership, and contributions so the service can work. Do not submit sensitive personal information. Account data is protected by Supabase Row Level Security and is only exposed where a feature requires sharing.

Community guidelines

  • Share accurate, useful, course-related material.
  • Be respectful and do not harass, impersonate, or target other students.
  • Do not post answer keys, stolen paid material, personal data, malware, or unsafe links.
  • Use reports for broken, outdated, incorrect, unsafe, or copyright-infringing resources.
  • Teachers and group leaders are responsible for how they use class join codes.

Copyright and takedowns

Only upload work you own or have permission to share. Resource reports include a copyright option so disputed material can be reviewed and removed. Add a monitored contact address before publishing this service publicly.

Acceptable use

Do not abuse authentication, scrape private data, evade moderation, automate spam, or attempt to access another person’s records. Accounts and contributions that threaten the safety or reliability of the community may be restricted.

Educational notice

AP Study Hub is a community study tool, not an official College Board service. Resources and practice questions should be independently reviewed for accuracy. AP and related marks belong to their respective owners.

Contact
