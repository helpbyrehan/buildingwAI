Community.

Plain-language expectations for using AP Study Hub responsibly.

Privacy

AP Study Hub stores account information, selected courses, study progress, saves, practice results, class membership, teacher applications and their review status, and contributions so the service can work. Do not submit sensitive personal information. Account data is protected by Supabase Row Level Security and is only exposed where a feature requires sharing.

College application tracker

The college tracker saves colleges, dates, tasks, essays, notes, and cost estimates in this browser by default. Guest and signed-in account workspaces are separate. If you enable account sync, that account’s tracker is uploaded to its private Supabase workspace, protected by Row Level Security. Turning sync off keeps the previous account copy and the browser copy. Tracker content is not sent to Study AI. Browser storage is not a secure document vault; do not store passwords, identification documents, or financial account details. Backups include your notes and essay drafts, so keep them private. Clearing browser data can remove local tracking information.

Community guidelines

  • Share accurate, useful, course-related material.
  • Be respectful and do not harass, impersonate, or target other students.
  • Do not post answer keys, stolen paid material, personal data, malware, or unsafe links.
  • Use reports for broken, outdated, incorrect, unsafe, or copyright-infringing resources.
  • Approved teachers and admins are responsible for how they use class join codes.

Copyright and takedowns

Only upload work you own or have permission to share. Resource reports include a copyright option so disputed material can be reviewed and removed. Add a monitored contact address before publishing this service publicly.

Acceptable use

Do not abuse authentication, scrape private data, evade moderation, automate spam, or attempt to access another person’s records. Accounts and contributions that threaten the safety or reliability of the community may be restricted.

Educational notice

AP Study Hub is a community study tool, not an official College Board service. Resources and practice questions should be independently reviewed for accuracy. AP and related marks belong to their respective owners.
